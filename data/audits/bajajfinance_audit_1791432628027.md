# Instagram Content Audit Report: @bajajfinance

**Audit date:** 8 October 2026 (UTC; connector snapshot approximately 04:07 UTC)  
**Account:** @bajajfinance — Bajaj Finance Limited  
**Sample:** 20 most recent posts returned by the Instagram connector, 17 September–6 October 2026 (UTC); insights retrieved for all 20.  
**Scope:** Two-part audit requested: T-2 post diagnostic and 20-post account strategy scorecard.

## Executive conclusion

The account is publishing frequently and its sampled feed is overwhelmingly video-led (18/20), with a strong human-interest Reel and a fraud-awareness Reel showing that emotional relevance and clear risk scenarios can travel much further than routine product explainers. However, the sample does not establish that the required carousel cadence is being met; captions frequently direct viewers to a bio link or product information rather than a single measurable response; comments are nearly absent outside two posts; and reach-based interaction performance is highly concentrated in one viral post. Strategy should retain high-performing stories and fraud education while adding verifiable, saveable financial explainers, an observable carousel lane, prompt-based CTAs, and formal claim/disclaimer review. Visual design, opening seconds, on-screen legal text, spoken audio, and retention drop-offs cannot be judged from the connector’s text and aggregate insight fields.

## SECTION 1: T-2 (48 HOURS AGO) POST DIAGNOSTIC

**T-2 convention used:** T-2 means the UTC calendar date 6 October 2026, two calendar dates before the audit date. One post was returned for that date. Its timestamp is **6 October 2026, 16:54:47 UTC**—about 35 hours 13 minutes before the connector snapshot, not exactly 48 hours before it. No post was published at the precise audit-time-minus-48-hours instant; the diagnostic below covers the post on the T-2 calendar date. This distinction avoids implying a 48-hour-old performance cohort that the timestamps do not support.

**T-2 post:** Reel, “Bajaj Finance CA Loan explore kar rahe hain? Apply karne se pehle kuch important cheezein jaan lena useful ho sakta hai.” [View Reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeKQj7dtoPb/)  
**Snapshot metrics:** 72 likes, 0 comments, 5 saves, 1 share; 82 reported interactions; reach 19,816; views 28,065. Interaction rate by reach: **0.41%**. Views/reach: **1.42** (a ratio, not a retention measure).

### 1. What Worked (Hooks, format, early reach)

- The format is a Reel, aligned to a video-first sample and compatible with discovery, although actual distribution sources were not returned.
- The caption opens with a defined product decision—exploring a CA loan—and a pre-application information promise. This is more targeted than a generic greeting and addresses a high-intent audience.
- The post accumulated 19,816 reach and 28,065 views at the available snapshot. Five saves indicate some reference intent, but the available metrics do not establish retention or whether this is “early” relative to other posts.

### 2. What Did Not Work (Drop-offs, weak retention, low comments)

- The observed interaction rate by reach is **0.41%**, below the sample pooled rate of 2.17% and the median per-post rate of 0.55%. These are descriptive comparisons, not causal benchmarks; the sample-wide mean is distorted by a viral outlier.
- No comments and only one share suggest little visible conversation or forwarding at the snapshot; five saves indicate limited save activity relative to 19,816 reach.
- Retention drop-offs, average watch time, completion rate, and first-2.5-second hold are unavailable. The view/reach ratio cannot substitute for retention or prove repeat viewing.
- The retrieved caption excerpt points viewers toward more information / a link in bio rather than a clearly evidenced single keyword, binary prompt, or save/comment action. As caption retrieval was truncated, this CTA assessment is limited to the returned text.

### 3. Root-Cause Analysis (Why lift or drop occurred)

- **Likely opportunity, not proven cause:** the product-specific “before applying” frame has relevance for CA borrowers, but the broad promise (“important things”) does not disclose the immediate payoff in the opening caption. A concrete checklist or decision rule could increase perceived utility.
- The post is a product explainer in a feed where the best sampled performance comes from a relatable human story and a fear-reversal fraud scenario. A utility-first product post may need a sharper problem, transparent worked example, and participation trigger to earn saves/shares.
- Observed engagement is sparse, especially comments. The metrics alone cannot distinguish creative weakness from audience targeting, post age, distribution, or platform factors; no historical time-series, impressions sources, or retention data were returned.
- Compliance and execution cannot be concluded from caption metrics: no claim review of the video frames, offer terms, audio, or on-screen qualifications was possible.

### 4. Strategic Adjustments (2-3 direct actionable pivots)

1. **Replace the broad intro with a concrete decision hook:** test “CA loan lene se pehle yeh 3 documents check karein” (define any technical term in-frame), then deliver exactly three practical points and one action. Success measures: 3-second hold, completion rate, saves/reach, and shares/reach.
2. **Turn the advice into a saveable companion carousel:** one CA-loan readiness checklist, one step per slide, swipe prompt on slides 2–8, final `SAVE` or `Comment CA` CTA. Include a relevant application/document caveat reviewed by compliance. Measure carousel completion/swipe-through, saves/reach, and keyword comments.
3. **Replace passive “learn more” direction with one measurable CTA and a direct prompt:** e.g., “Comment CA for the document checklist” or “Save this before applying.” A/B-test one CTA at a time; compare qualified comments and saves per reach, not likes alone.

## SECTION 2: 20-POST ACCOUNT AUDIT & STRATEGY SCORECARD

### Format mix, cadence, and feed positioning

- **Format mix:** 18 video posts (90%) and 2 image posts (10%). The connector did not mark whether either image post is a carousel. Therefore the sample supports a minimum **2 Reels/video posts per full calendar week** in the observed weeks, but cannot verify the required **1 Carousel per week**. Two image posts are not enough evidence to call either carousel or static single-image content.
- **Posting cadence by UTC calendar week (ISO):** 17–20 Sep: 4 posts; 21–27 Sep: 7; 28 Sep–4 Oct: 7; 5–11 Oct through the 6 Oct snapshot: 2. All observed weeks have at least two video posts, but the last week is incomplete. Volume is high relative to the user’s minimum and should not be increased further until format balance and post utility improve.
- **Content lanes observed from returned captions:** product promotion / product utility is prominent (CA, business, home, gold, solar, car, securities and EMI); fraud awareness appears in two clear examples; human-interest/brand purpose appears in two image posts and one high-performing Reel; entertainment/series appears in the rapid-fire episode. No reliable full-content lane classification is possible for every caption because some returned excerpts are shortened.
- **Brand role and positioning:** product utility connects readily to the finance/NBFC category, while the human-interest swim-story and ambition Reel create emotional reach but need an explicit, natural bridge to financial inclusion or the brand promise. The connector’s current bio (“For all your financial needs”) is less distinctive than the user-supplied brand context. Align the live profile bio and pinned/highlight structure to the approved promise, subject to brand ownership.
- **Hook evidence:** captions include direct dilemmas (“applying?”, “plan hai?”), corrections (“subsidy… solar free nahi”), a panic-warning KYC hook, and a human-story tension (“yeh tumse nahi hoga”). The human story drove the strongest observed response; the KYC warning led the remaining sample on reported interactions (428) and shares (38). Caption hooks are observable; visual/audio pattern interrupts and first-frame execution are not.
- **Engagement quality:** sample median comments is zero. The human-interest Reel generated 242 comments; a Solar Finance explainer generated 21 and a different home-loan story had 1. Most posts did not elicit a comment. The most viral Reel contributed 4,987 shares and 1,535 saves; across the other 19 posts, average interactions were 226. Build reusable participation into routine education rather than expecting product mentions alone to create discussion.
- **Accessibility and jargon:** terms visible in captions include EMI, ROI, subsidy, KYC, loan against securities, working capital, and financial product names. In the ROI explainer, the caption helpfully expands ROI as “Rate of Interest”; maintain that standard and add plain-language spoken/on-screen definitions. Explain EMI as the fixed periodic loan payment (with principal/interest context where relevant), and distinguish subsidy from total installed cost.
- **Claim/disclaimer screening:** captions refer to approval, borrowing, subsidy, eligibility, cost, “extra” festive/OTT benefits, and product coverage/benefits. These require claim-specific review, particularly a subsidy/cost explanation, approval-related wording, eligibility, partner benefits, product coverage, rates or absolute amounts if shown in the creative. The returned caption excerpts do not establish whether there is a proximate, legible legal qualification on-screen. Absence in a caption excerpt is **not** evidence that the video has no on-screen disclaimer. Obtain frame-level review and compliance sign-off before asserting adequacy.
- **Single-concept, CTA, opening, and visual guardrails:** captions are generally focused on one product or story; however, this cannot verify that each edit sticks to one concept. The sampled text includes practical and product-led CTAs, but several rely on link-in-bio or broad “learn more” directions; captions should end with a binary diagnostic prompt or one keyword/action. No caption excerpts suggest the prohibited “Murukku vs Chakli: The Crunch Audit” theme. Overlay contrast, 4–5-word text bursts, greetings in the actual first frame, and swipe prompts cannot be audited without creative assets.

### Recommended 30-day test plan

1. **Weeks 1–4: format balance.** Publish at least 2 Reels plus 1 explicitly labeled carousel weekly. Keep image posts identifiable in the publishing log as single-image or carousel.
2. **Hook test.** For comparable financial topics, alternate dilemma-led and misconception-led openings; maintain the same CTA and approximate duration. Record 2.5-second hold, completion, saves/reach, shares/reach, and comments/reach at fixed time checkpoints.
3. **CTA test.** Compare a single `SAVE` CTA with a single keyword-comment CTA across similar educational posts; do not change hook and CTA simultaneously. Track qualified actions per reach.
4. **Math-and-compliance test.** Publish a practical example with stated assumptions, formula, transparent calculation, plain-language glossary, and approved on-screen/caption qualifications. Review all claim-bearing edits before release.
5. **Reporting.** At 24h and 72h, report median interaction rate by reach by format and lane, plus saves/reach, shares/reach, and comments/reach. Flag virality separately so outliers do not mask typical performance.

## Final assessment

Keep the account’s video cadence and storytelling strengths, but make useful financial learning—not product exposure alone—the repeatable content engine. A weekly observable carousel, arithmetic-backed explainers, concrete hooks, one-action CTAs, and claim-by-claim compliance review are the highest-value pivots. Judge outcomes by fixed-window medians and retention metrics, not raw views or a single viral outlier.

## Method and limitations

- **Data source:** Instagram connector account-info, recent-post list, and post-insights tools for the configured @bajajfinance account. Recent-post sample is the 20 entries returned; all 20 returned post IDs had insights with likes, comments, saves, shares, total interactions, reach, and views.
- **Date/time:** Post timestamps supplied as UTC. T-2 is interpreted as the UTC calendar date 6 October 2026. The sampled post on that date was posted at 16:54:47 UTC, about 35h13 before the snapshot at approximately 04:08 UTC on 8 October; it was not exactly 48 hours old at snapshot time.
- **Calculations:** Pooled interaction rate = sum of reported interactions ÷ sum of reach. Per-post rates use reported interactions ÷ post reach. Connector total interactions reconciled with likes + comments + saves + shares on sampled records. Views/reach is not a retention measure.
- **Unavailable evidence:** No individual post creation assets, carousel slide counts, first frames, video playback, audio, transcript, on-screen text/disclaimer legibility, retention/watch-time, saves-source, audience demographics, distribution-source breakdown, or profile conversion insights were exposed in retrieved data. Captions in the connector output were sometimes shortened; CTA and claim observations are limited to visible returned excerpts. No visual compliance conclusions are made.
- **Benchmark context:** historical ER and competitor figures in the user’s prompt were treated as supplied context, not independently verified or normalized. They are not directly comparable without matching dates, denominators, formats, and sampling.
- **Compliance:** This is a content-risk screen, not legal advice or approval. Any financial, product, offer, partner, eligibility, rate, amount, benefit, or outcome claim should receive the organization’s normal compliance review.
