## Format mix, content lanes, hooks, jargon, and compliance

### Format mix

Public profile rendering labels at least seven visible items as videos and at least three as photos, with one additional older photo reference. This suggests a Reel-led mix, but it is not a verified 20-post denominator. A complete audit should report exact percentages for Reels, carousels, static posts, and other formats after connector access is corrected.

### Content lanes

The visible evidence supports three probable lanes: **local-store/community**, **customer-love and humour**, and **snack/product discovery**. The account should formalize these into recurring series so followers understand what to expect: for example, “Snack of the Week,” “Electronic City Quick Bite,” “Traditional vs Modern,” and “What’s Fresh Today.”

### Hooks

Two publicly readable hooks are direct and conversational: “Looks like someone forgot something...” and “Plot twist: Every customer is our favorite customer.” These are useful attention hooks because they create curiosity and warmth. They are weaker educational or conversion hooks because they do not immediately specify the snack, offer, flavour, price, availability, or next action. A stronger pattern would combine curiosity with product utility: “Can you identify this South Indian crunch in one bite?” followed by the product reveal and a DM/order CTA.

### Accessibility and jargon

No technical finance jargon was identified in the accessible Snacc Mart captions. Food-specific terms should still be made concrete where needed: name the snack, flavour, spice level, serving size, and availability in plain language. Add on-screen text for product names and location because visual/audio execution could not be verified from text-only evidence.

### Fraud-awareness lane

No fraud-awareness content was verified in the accessible Snacc Mart evidence. For this food-retail brand, a light trust-and-safety lane may still be useful if relevant to actual operations: official ordering links, payment-scam warnings, and a clear statement of official contact/order channels. This should not displace the core snack proposition.

### Compliance and disclaimers

No specific financial claim was verified for @snacc.mart in the accessible evidence. The supplied financial-claim rules should remain a guardrail if the account later promotes financing, partner offers, cashback, investment-like claims, or absolute monetary benefits. Caption evidence alone cannot establish whether on-screen legal qualification is present; legal review is required for any such creative.
