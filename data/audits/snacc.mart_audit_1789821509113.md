### What appears to be working

**The profile proposition is legible.** The name, bio, location, and product categories quickly tell a local viewer what the business sells and where it is. The link hub and highlights for Zomato and Swiggy create a plausible path from discovery to ordering.

**The account uses a discovery-friendly format.** Eight of the 10 surfaced 2026 posts were Reels/videos. That is a sensible format for showing texture, crunch, preparation, serving moments, store atmosphere, and local personality. It should be retained, but paired with saveable carousels and stronger conversion prompts.

**Local relevance is an asset.** Search-visible wording connected the account with Electronic City, Bengaluru, Bangalore food, snack time, and shop-local behavior. Local cues can outperform generic food content when paired with a specific visit or ordering action.

**The category permits high-contrast demonstrations.** Chips breaking, chaat assembly, drink pours, masala coating, freshness checks, and “choose one” comparisons are naturally visual pattern interrupts. These are strong foundations for the supplied overlay and first-2.5-second rules.

### What is not working or is underdeveloped

**The measurement layer is not audit-ready.** The connected Instagram account is @bajajfinance, while the requested handle is @snacc.mart. This prevents reliable post-level insight retrieval and makes any performance conclusion unsafe.

**The visible feed lacks an observable save/share architecture.** No carousel was surfaced in the 2026 public sample, and no consistent save, order, DM, or keyword CTA was visible in the available metadata. Product content should tell the viewer exactly what to do next.

**Format variety is narrow.** An 80% Reel / 20% photo mix can support reach but leaves little structured education. Introduce carousels for snack comparisons, ingredient or freshness education, serving pairings, “what to order for two,” and store/order instructions.

**Entertainment needs a product bridge.** Meme and ambience content can build familiarity, but each post should resolve into a product, location, ordering, or community action. “Good snacks” alone is not a conversion framework.

**The brief contains a brand-strategy contradiction.** The requested bio and public profile are snack retail; the supplied category is Finance & Technology and the rules emphasize finance claims, EMI arithmetic, and fraud awareness. Those rules should not be mechanically applied to a snack account. The operating brief should be reconciled before the next content sprint.

### Recommended 30-day content test plan

**Week 1: Baseline and instrumentation.** Connect the correct account, export the latest 20 posts, and tag each by format, topic, hook, CTA, product, local cue, and objective. Establish baseline reach rate, three-second hold, average watch time, saves per reach, shares per reach, comments per reach, profile actions, and link taps.

**Week 2: Hook test.** Publish three Reels testing distinct openings: a sensory break/crunch, a bold choice (“Chaat or chips?”), and a local-intent prompt (“Electronic City snack stop”). Keep product, duration, and CTA as constant as practical.

**Week 3: Utility test.** Publish two carousels: “What to order for two” and “Hot chips vs chaat: choose by craving.” Use slide 2–8 swipe triggers and a single final CTA: **SAVE** or **COMMENT ORDER**.

**Week 4: Conversion test.** Publish a product Reel with one local action and a photo with one ordering action. Compare DM keyword, profile tap, link tap, and direction behavior against awareness-only posts. Keep the winning hook and CTA combination for the next month.

### Final assessment

@snacc.mart has a credible local snack-retail proposition and a visually suitable category for high-contrast, sensory short-form video. The next improvement is operational: connect the exact account, measure the right events, enforce one concept and one CTA per post, and add a carousel utility layer. Finance-specific rules should be retained only if the requested brand context is corrected; otherwise, replace them with food-safety, offer, delivery, and local-commerce governance appropriate to Snacc Mart.
